Alexis Puebla

Alexis Puebla is a Mexican multidisciplinary creative with a focus in Fashion and Design and a minor in Graphic Design at Queens College. Born and raised in the heavily diversified Jackson Heights, they were exposed to many cultures and peoples, influencing their appreciation and interest in global arts. The neighborhood is a reference point that is reflected in his personal work. During his college years, he has worked as the graphic designer for the Fashion Design club, creating posters. Along with that, he’s worked as the social media manager for the Fashion and Design program’s Instagram page. Here, Alexis created layouts, filmed and edited video reels capturing the stories of students’ works, diversity, and the courses, expanding the programs outreach and increasing enrollment. The projects that are most intriguing to Alexis are fashion related, text based motion graphics, experimental animation, and poster design. When he’s not working he’s probably biking!
